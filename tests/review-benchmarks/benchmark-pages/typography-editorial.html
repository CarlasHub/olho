Enterprise design quality depends on how quickly reviewers can understand visual evidence

Design review tools often fail because they treat screenshots as raw artefacts rather than communication surfaces. A professional reviewer needs structure, rhythm, evidence, and prioritisation. When lines are too long and line-height is too tight, readers must track across wide spans while holding context in working memory, which makes the critique harder to absorb and reduces confidence in the report.

Related notes, context, audit details, status, metadata, review version, ownership, and historical decisions all compete in a narrow area.
