Privacy

Olho runs locally in your browser. Captures and recordings are stored in local IndexedDB and stay under your control.

  • No analytics
  • No cloud sync
  • No accounts
  • No remote logging

Permission Explanations

  • activeTab: capture the current tab when you click capture actions.
  • tabs: open Olho pages (editor, gallery, recorder, reports).
  • scripting: draw capture overlays and full-page progress on target pages.
  • clipboardWrite: copy local files to clipboard when you explicitly choose copy actions.
  • storage: save settings and local media metadata.
  • desktopCapture: start screen/tab/window recording through browser picker.
  • downloads: export files to your Downloads folder when you choose export.
  • offscreen: run local offscreen DOM processing for video poster thumbnails.
