Never Uploaded

Olho does not upload files, create hosted links, or sync to cloud services.

No Analytics

You Control Deletion

Delete items or clear local data from Memory and Settings at any time.

What Olho stores

  • Screenshots and recordings are saved in local IndexedDB Blob storage.
  • Settings are saved locally in your browser profile.
  • Media is never uploaded by Olho.
  • Olho does not upload screenshots or recordings to any server.
  • No cloud upload or hosted share links are provided.
  • You can export before delete and remove all local data at any time.

What Olho does not do

  • No telemetry.
  • No analytics.
  • No remote logging.
  • No account system.
  • No background upload jobs.

Permission use in plain language

activeTab

Capture the page you explicitly choose.

tabs

Open Olho pages such as editor, Memory, recorder, and Export.

scripting

Run capture overlays and full-page progress scripts on chosen pages.

clipboardWrite

Copy local files only when you trigger copy actions.

sidePanel

Open the local Review panel beside the page you explicitly review.

desktopCapture

Open browser picker for screen, tab, or window recording.

downloads

Save exported files to your local disk.

Data lifecycle reminders

Clearing browser profile data can remove your local Olho library.

Export files before cleanup if you need backups.
